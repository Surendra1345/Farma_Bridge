# Farma Bridge: knowledge base for the in-app assistant
(Keep this file accurate when features change. The assistant may only answer from it.)

## What Farma Bridge is
Farma Bridge is a local agriculture marketplace. It connects farmers, buyers, machinery owners and storage providers in one place.
- Farmers sell crops (vegetables, fruits, grains and more).
- Machinery owners rent out equipment such as tractors, harvesters and drones.
- Storage providers rent out space such as cold rooms and warehouses.
- Buyers order crops and book machinery or storage.
- An AI crop-photo check ("Crop Doctor") inside this chat can look at photos of a sick plant and suggest what is wrong.
The app is a meeting place and record-keeper. It does not deliver goods and it does not handle money.

## Who can do what
- Anyone, without signing in: browse and search crops, machinery and storage; use this chat to ask about the app.
- Signed-in users: sell, order, book, see the owner's phone number with Call and WhatsApp buttons, review people, and check crop photos in this chat.
- A person can have several roles at once: Farmer, Buyer, Machinery owner, Storage owner. Roles are chosen when creating the account and can be added later.

## Signing in and creating an account
- There is no password. You sign in with your email: enter it, then type the 6-digit one-time code that is emailed to you.
- Codes last 5 minutes, allow 5 wrong tries, and a new code can be requested after about 30 seconds. If no code arrives, check the spam folder and wait a little before asking for another.
- To create an account, tap "Create an account" in the sign-in box. Enter email, name, a 10-digit phone number, address and village/town, and pick your roles.
- Tapping Sign in (top right), or any action that needs an account (Buy, Book, Start selling), opens the sign-in box.

## Browsing
- The home page has three sections: Crops, Machinery and Storage (top bar on desktop, bottom bar on phones).
- Use the search box to search by name or location. Crops can also be filtered by category (Vegetables, Fruits, Grains).
- Each card shows the price, location and availability. Signed-in users also see WhatsApp and Call buttons on cards.

## Selling a crop (Farmer)
1. Sign in, then tap "Start selling" on the home page (or Post on phones).
2. Choose Crop. Fill in crop name, category, optional grade, total quantity (kg), price per kg, location, and add a photo (photo is required).
3. Tap Publish listing. The Farmer role is added automatically if you do not have it.
- Crop listings expire after 5 days for perishable crops and 30 days for others. Editing an expired listing renews it. Expired listings disappear from the marketplace and cannot be ordered.
- Manage your listings in Account > My listings: edit, change status (Available, Partially Sold, Sold) or delete. A listing that already has orders cannot be deleted; mark it Sold to hide it.
- Photos must be JPEG, PNG or WEBP, up to 5 MB.

## Renting out machinery or storage
- Machinery: Start selling > Machinery. Enter machine type (for example Tractor), charge unit (Per Hour or Per Acre), price, location and a photo.
- Storage: Start selling > Storage. Enter storage type (for example Cold room), capacity, charge unit (Per Day, Per Week or Per Month), price, location and a photo.
- The owner can mark machinery as Booked, or storage as Full, to stop new requests.

## Buying crops
1. Tap "Buy / contact grower" on a crop card, enter the quantity, and tap Confirm order. The total is calculated for you.
2. The farmer sees your order under Account > Requests and can Accept or Decline. Stock is reduced when the farmer accepts.
3. You can cancel a Pending order. After acceptance, either side can mark it Completed when done.
- You can have only one Pending order per listing at a time. You cannot order your own listing, or more than the available stock.

## Booking machinery or storage
1. Tap "Book machinery" or "Book storage", choose the From and Until date and time, and tap Request booking.
2. For hourly, daily, weekly or monthly rates the price is worked out from your dates. For Per Acre machinery you enter how many acres, and the price is rate x acres.
3. The owner accepts or declines under Account > Requests.
- Rules: start time cannot be in the past; at most 365 days ahead; a booking can be at most 90 days long; it cannot overlap an already confirmed booking of the same item; you cannot hold two overlapping requests for the same item (cancel the first one to change dates).
- A booking can only be marked Completed after it has started.

## Talking to owners and sellers
- Signed-in users see WhatsApp and Call buttons on listing cards. WhatsApp opens a chat with a ready-made message.
- Once you order or request a booking, both people can see each other's phone number on the order/booking card (Call and WhatsApp buttons). Numbers are hidden again if the deal is cancelled.
- Phone numbers are never shown to people who are not signed in.
- There is no chat inside the app between buyers and sellers; use WhatsApp or a phone call.

## Payments
- Farma Bridge does not process money. Buyers pay sellers directly by UPI, cash or bank transfer.
- After an order or booking is Confirmed, the payer taps "I've paid" and chooses UPI, Cash, Bank transfer or Other. The seller then taps "Confirm received" (or "Not received" if the money did not arrive). The seller can also tap "Payment received" for cash. Either side can undo a mistake, and the other person is notified.
- Pay by QR code: sellers can add an optional UPI id in Account > Profile. Once a deal is confirmed, the buyer sees a QR code for the exact amount (scan with GPay, PhonePe, Paytm or any UPI app), the UPI id with a Copy button, and an "Open UPI app" button on phones. If the seller has not added a UPI id, ask them on WhatsApp or pay in cash.
- The payment status is only a record; it is not proof of payment.

## Managing requests
- Account > Requests shows requests on your own listings. Account > My orders shows what you ordered or booked.
- A badge on the bell icon shows how many requests are waiting for your answer. The app refreshes about every 30 seconds.
- Sellers: Accept or Decline new requests, then Mark completed when finished. Buyers can Cancel Pending requests.

## Reviews and ratings
- After an order or booking is Completed, both people see a Rate button (1 to 5 stars, optional comment).
- You can review only the other person in your own completed deal, once per deal.
- You can edit or delete your own review for 7 days. A person's average rating appears next to their name on orders and bookings.

## Reporting a problem user
- On an order or booking card, tap "Report" next to the other person, pick a reason, add details and send. Admins review reports.

## Your account
- Account > Profile: change name, phone, address, village/town and optional UPI id. Email cannot be changed.
- Roles: each active role has a "Turn off" link, and "Add" brings it back. A role cannot be turned off while it is in use (open orders or bookings, crop listings not marked Sold, machinery not marked Booked, storage not marked Full). The app tells you what to finish first.
- Delete my account (bottom of Profile): erases your name, phone and email and takes your listings off the marketplace. It is blocked while you have open orders or bookings, and it cannot be undone.
- Admins (a few trusted people chosen by the site owner) can resolve reports and suspend users.

## Crop photo check (Crop Doctor), inside this chat
- Tap the photo button next to the message box and choose 1 to 3 photos of the sick plant (close-up of the affected leaf plus the whole plant works best, in daylight and in focus). You must be signed in; if you are not, the chat will ask you to sign in.
- You can type the crop name and what you noticed (when it started, rain, sprays) together with the photos, and choose the reply language (English, Telugu or Hindi) in the chat header.
- Result: what looks wrong (disease, pest, nutrient or water stress) with confidence and severity, why it happens, what to do now, organic and chemical options (names only, never doses; always follow the product label), prevention, tips for better photos, and when to see an agriculture officer or Krishi Vigyan Kendra.
- If the photos are unclear it says so and asks for better ones. The result can be shared on WhatsApp.
- Photos are resized, stripped of camera and GPS data, sent to an AI service (Groq) for that one check, and are not saved by Farma Bridge.
- Limit: 10 checks per hour per signed-in user. It is AI guidance from photos, not a lab test or a field visit; for serious or spreading problems see an agriculture officer.

## What Farma Bridge does not do
- No online payment, escrow, refunds or invoices; no delivery or transport; no price negotiation tool; no in-app messaging between users.
- No weather, mandi/market price information, or general farming advice outside the crop photo check.
- The assistant cannot place orders, book, change settings, or see anyone's account or listings. It only explains the app.

## Language
- The app screens are in English. This chat understands English, Telugu and Hindi and replies in the language you write in. Crop photo results can be in English, Telugu or Hindi.

## Privacy
- Anyone can browse without signing in. Phone numbers are only shown to signed-in users (on listings) and to the two people in a deal. A seller's UPI id is shown only to the person paying, while the deal is confirmed or completed.

## Quick troubleshooting
- No sign-in code: check spam, wait 30 seconds, request again.
- Cannot see Call/WhatsApp: sign in first.
- Cannot book: check dates are in the future, within 90 days length and not overlapping a confirmed booking; for Per Acre items enter the acres.
- Cannot order: you may already have a Pending order on that listing, the listing may be sold out or expired, or it may be your own.
- Cannot turn a role off: finish open orders/bookings and mark listings Sold / Booked / Full first.
- Photo check says "sign in": photo checks need an account; chatting about the app does not.
